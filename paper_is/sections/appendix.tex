Differences caused by \texttt{NULL} handling, by filters and by join paths account for about two thirds of the sampled witnesses. Ordering and \texttt{LIMIT} ties, duplicates and aggregates over empty inputs account for most of the rest. These are also the classes in which the AI attribution most often finds implausible data, such as \texttt{NULL} in always-filled fields or duplicated entities, or ambiguous questions, as with ties.

\section{Deviations from the pre-registrations}\label{app:deviations}
This appendix lists the deviations from the pre-registrations that Section~\ref{sec:threats} summarizes.
\begin{itemize}
\item[(i)] The attribution of first-stage witnesses was added after the first test run and replaced a pre-registered residual audit of undisproved pairs.
\item[(ii)] The pre-registered manual spot check of rule-based root-cause labels was replaced by two AI passes after an AI check found the rule-based labels imprecise. No human check of root causes was made.
\item[(iii)] Before the second freeze, the definition of $\mathcal A_R$ in the second pre-registration was revised on development data. The observed-value condition was restricted to categorical columns, measure columns were excluded from unique column combinations, and the value-dependency class was added. The record of this revision was written after the second test run, but the changed files are covered by the freeze hashes.
\item[(iv)] The second stage was designed after seeing first-stage test attributions (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}).
\item[(v)] Stage~3 (Sections~\ref{sec:replication} and~\ref{sec:validity}) was pre-registered before any of its data were read. At that time, the round-1 sheets were complete but unread. The repair test was added post hoc, after the first human round, and its procedure was changed once after a 12-item smoke run. A build fix for two non-UTF-8 cells of one Spider database preceded all Spider runs, and part of one run shared the machine with analysis jobs. The SpotIt re-run ran in resumed chunks of at most 105 minutes, with each pair run once.
\item[(vi)] The pre-registrations are internally timestamped, not registered with an independent registry. Stages 1--3 are timestamped by commits of our development repository, and the later pre-registrations also by the public repository. The whole study took place within one week (Section~\ref{sec:protocol}). The released snapshot lets anyone verify the frozen method and baseline code against the recorded hashes. The six analysis-only scripts that changed after the freezes are listed, and the full commit history can be made available to the editors. The complete list of amendments, with timestamps, is in the artifact repository.
\item[(vii)] After an independent pre-submission review, we added post hoc analyses that were not pre-registered: a rerun of 500 subsample pairs, SpotIt with a 60\,s budget on 150 pairs, a leave-one-class-out ablation of the profile, H6 with entity-missing witnesses counted as not meaningful, duplication-weighted estimates, Wilson intervals, and the round-3 measures without annotator~A. They were defined before their results were seen, are labeled post hoc in the text, and are logged as amendment E20.
\end{itemize}
