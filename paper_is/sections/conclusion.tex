\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
A database on which a prediction and the gold query disagree is easy to misread. Under one certifier and budget, our disprover finds such databases for more accepted BIRD predictions than existing verifiers and test generators at DDL- and format-derived tiers. Yet almost none of these witnesses satisfies a profile of the benchmark data, and judging whether they are evidence is hard. An AI annotator and two humans shown facts about the data rejected most of them, but three further humans agreed with each other barely above chance. Searching real sub-databases first certifies more profile-tier witnesses than random fuzzing on BIRD and on unseen systems, but not on Spider's small instances. At the profile tier, witnesses lower EX by about one point per system if weighted by the AI's attributed error rates, by about three points with the round-3 human majority's rates, which are of limited reliability, and by ten to thirteen points if all are counted. We recommend that benchmarks publish their data profile. We also recommend that audits report certified witnesses together with the data facts they rest on and the agreement of their annotators. Future work should add conditional and presupposition constraints to the profile, cover other engines, and develop attribution protocols that humans can reproduce.
